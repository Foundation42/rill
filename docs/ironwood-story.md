## The Scenario: The Watch at Ironwood Keep
Ironwood Keep is a vital medieval border fort guarding a narrow mountain pass. A sudden dusk raid by enemy raiders triggers the castle's defense system, testing the garrison's readiness.
------------------------------
## Standing Orders

      [ THE KEEP ] (Garrison / Commander)
           ^
           | (Horn Signals / Beacons)
           v
   [ THE LOOKOUT TOWER ] ======> [ THE MAIN GATE ]
     (Sentries / Scouts)         (Gate Guards / Archers)

## 1. The Watchers (Sentries & Lookouts)

* Location: The high watchtower and wall walks.
* Standing Orders:
* Maintain a constant scan of the tree line and pass.
   * At the first sight of moving steel or dust clouds, sound the alarm horn (two long blasts for an approach, three short for an immediate threat).
   * If at night, ignite the iron beacon braziers immediately to alert the valley.
   * Do not leave the post to fight; your eyes are the keep's greatest weapon.

## 2. The Gate Guard & Wall Archers

* Location: The barbican, portcullis winch room, and battlements.
* Standing Orders:
* At the sound of the alarm, drop the portcullis (the iron grate) and raise the drawbridge.
   * Hold the gatehouse at all costs; do not open it for anyone once the alarm sounds.
   * Loose arrows at the enemy vanguard to slow their advance and buy time for the main force.

## 3. The Garrison (The Reaction Force)

* Location: The courtyard barracks and Great Hall.
* Standing Orders:
* Sleep in light gambesons (padded armour) with weapons within arm's reach.
   * Upon hearing the horn, assemble in the central courtyard within two minutes.
   * Form a shield wall facing the gatehouse, ready to repel a breach.
   * Await the Commander's order to open the sally port (the hidden side door) for a surprise counter-attack.

------------------------------
## An Example of What Happened: The Raid on Ironwood
The sun had just dipped below the mountains when Sentry Tom saw a flock of birds scatter from the forest a mile out. Peering through the dusk, he caught the glint of dying sunlight on iron chainmail. Raiders.
Tom blew two long, shattering blasts into his horn.

Down in the courtyard, the silence broke. The garrison soldiers dropped their dice and supper bowls. They grabbed spears, bucklers, and helmets, sprinting into the open air. Within ninety seconds, thirty men stood in a tight, bristling shield wall under the banner of the Garrison Commander.
At the front gate, the guards cranked the heavy iron winch. The massive timber drawbridge groaned upward just as the first enemy horsemen thundered out of the trees. Arrows from the wall lookouts flew down, pinning the lead rider to the dirt.

The enemy attack stalled at the closed ditch. Seeing the raiders bunch up in confusion outside the walls, the Commander pointed to the side of the keep. "To the sally port!" he barked.

The garrison slipped out of the hidden, narrow side door in total silence. They hit the enemy’s exposed flank with a roar, catching the raiders between the castle walls and a wall of spears. By nightfall, the threat was broken, and the keep remained secure.
